Powerful Features

Everything you need to transform your phone into a controller

PC Mode

Connect over Wi-Fi, USB tethering, or Bluetooth using the Magic Gamepad Windows app for XInput support and the best compatibility with Windows games.

Bluetooth HID Mode

Connect directly to compatible Bluetooth-enabled devices without installing the Windows companion app. Works with Windows, Android, Android TV, Linux, and other Bluetooth HID hosts.

Multiplayer Support

Connect up to four phones simultaneously for local multiplayer gaming in both PC Mode and Bluetooth HID Mode.

Multiple Custom Layouts

Create and save multiple custom controller layouts, then switch between them anytime to match different games and play styles.

Motion & Analog Controls

Experience more immersive gameplay with sensor-based steering and smooth analog slider triggers for realistic driving and precise control.

Keyboard & Touchpad

Use your phone as a wireless keyboard and touchpad for easy navigation and media control.

How It Works

Get up and running in minutes with just five simple steps.

  1. 1

    Download the Mobile App

    Download MagicGamepad from the Google Play Store and install it on your Android device.

  2. 2

    Download the Desktop App

    Download the MagicGamepad desktop app for Windows from our website and install it on your PC.

  3. 3

    Connect Your Devices

    Connect your Android device to your PC using Wi-Fi, USB tethering, hotspot, or Bluetooth. Follow our easy setup guide to get started quickly.

  4. 4

    Select Your Controller Number

    Choose your player number to ensure seamless multiplayer gaming. Connect up to 4 devices for an engaging experience.

  5. 5

    Start Playing!

    Start gaming, controlling media, or navigating your PC with ease using MagicGamepad.
